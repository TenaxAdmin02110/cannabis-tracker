                  How this tracker works

                  Status: "Retail" means adult-use cannabis is legal under state law (Virginia's retail sales start July 1, 2027; DC sales are limited by a congressional rider). "Medical only" means a comprehensive medical program (Texas's is limited). "Banned" means no comprehensive medical or adult-use program, though some of these states allow low-THC/CBD products. Election winners come from media projections (AP, CBS News) and are unofficial until states certify them. Odds are Tenax's analytical estimates from the reporting linked on each card and should be read as directional, not precise. News: each story links to the outlet, agency, law firm or advocacy group that published it (no forums or social media); the short summary under each headline describes the article itself. Upcoming changes: when you open a state with a dated change in the next 90 days, a "Coming up" card shows first, with a source link for each item; close it to see the full state page. Municipal status comes from a copy of Tenax's municipal tracker (Airtable) for the states it covers; for every other state it comes from that state's own official source (a regulator's license list, open-data file, or statute), linked on each area. Where state law, not local choice, decides the answer (banned states, Oklahoma, Texas, Nebraska and DC), every county is shaded with the governing statute. Map boundaries, Interstates and city points come from the U.S. Census Bureau (2024).
